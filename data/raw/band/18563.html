A Blind Prophecy

A Blind Prophecy consisted of teenagers from Northeastern Rhode Island with a strong desire to create and play melodic metal.

The group played live shows around Southeastern New England at clubs like Jarrod's Place (Attleboro, MA), The Living Room (Providence, RI), and The Dropzone (Cumberland, RI).

The group disbanded when guitarist & song writer Ben Fortier joined the US Marine Corps ...
