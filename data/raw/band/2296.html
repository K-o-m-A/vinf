A Canorous Quintet

Country of origin:
Sweden
Location:
Kungsängen, Stockholm
Status:
Active
Formed in:
1991
Genre:
Melodic Death Metal
Themes:
Death, Anxiety, Negativity, Darkness, Hopelessness
Current label:
Black Lodge Records
Years active:
1991-1993 (as A Canorous Quartet), 1993-1999, 2016-present
They started as A Canorous Quartet in 1991, but changed their name to A Canorous Quintet in 1993 to reflect their change in number of band members.

Split up in 1999. All members of the last lineup went on to form The Plague, which later became This Ending. However, A Canorous Quintet re-activated in 2016 and both bands are now active at the same time.
